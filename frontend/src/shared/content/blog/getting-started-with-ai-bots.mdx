## Introduction

AI-powered bots are revolutionizing how businesses interact with customers. In this comprehensive guide, we'll walk you through everything you need to know to build your first bot.

## Why Build a Bot?

Bots can help you:

- **Automate repetitive tasks** - Save hours of manual work
- **Provide 24/7 support** - Never miss a customer inquiry
- **Scale your operations** - Handle thousands of conversations simultaneously
- **Improve user experience** - Instant responses and personalized interactions

## Prerequisites

Before we begin, make sure you have:

1. A Viably account (sign up for free)
2. Basic understanding of your use case
3. 5 minutes of your time

## Step 1: Choose Your Platform

Viably supports multiple messaging platforms:

- **Telegram** - Popular in Eastern Europe and Asia
- **Discord** - Perfect for gaming communities
- **Slack** - Ideal for workplace automation
- **WhatsApp** - Reach 2 billion users worldwide

For this tutorial, we'll use Telegram due to its developer-friendly API.

## Step 2: Create Your Bot

```typescript
// Example bot configuration
const botConfig = {
  name: "Customer Support Bot",
  platform: "telegram",
  personality: "friendly and helpful",
  capabilities: ["answer FAQ", "book appointments", "escalate to human"]
}
```

Navigate to your Viably dashboard and click "Create New Bot". Fill in the details:

1. **Bot Name**: Choose something descriptive
2. **Platform**: Select Telegram
3. **Purpose**: Describe what your bot should do
4. **Tone**: Set the conversation style

## Step 3: Configure AI Personality

The AI personality is crucial for user experience. Consider:

- **Tone**: Professional, casual, or playful?
- **Language**: Formal or conversational?
- **Expertise**: What domain knowledge does it need?

## Step 4: Test Your Bot

Before deploying, thoroughly test your bot:

```bash
# Send test messages
/start
Hello, how can you help me?
What are your business hours?
```

## Step 5: Deploy and Monitor

Once testing is complete, deploy your bot with one click. Monitor performance through the Viably dashboard:

- Response accuracy
- Average response time
- User satisfaction scores
- Conversation volume

## Best Practices

### 1. Start Simple

Don't try to handle every scenario on day one. Start with common queries and expand gradually.

### 2. Provide Fallbacks

Always have a way to escalate to human support when needed:

```typescript
if (userFrustrationLevel > 3) {
  escalateToHuman()
}
```

### 3. Iterate Based on Data

Review conversation logs regularly to identify:

- Common questions you're missing
- Confusing bot responses
- Opportunities for improvement

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Over-promising capabilities** - Be clear about what your bot can and cannot do
2. **Ignoring context** - Make sure your bot remembers previous messages
3. **Poor error handling** - Always provide helpful messages when something goes wrong
4. **Forgetting about privacy** - Handle user data responsibly

## Next Steps

- Creating custom actions and integrations
