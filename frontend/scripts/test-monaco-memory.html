Monaco Editor Memory Leak Test

⚠️ Important: Open DevTools First

Press F12 or Cmd+Option+I to open DevTools and navigate to the Memory tab before starting the test.

📋 Testing Instructions

  1. Open Chrome DevTools → Memory tab
  2. Click "1. Capture Baseline" below and take a heap snapshot in DevTools
  3. Click "2. Open Editor" and wait for Monaco to load
  4. Click "3. Capture With Editor" and take another heap snapshot
  5. Click "4. Close Editor" to dispose the editor
  6. Click "5. Force GC" multiple times (3-5 times)
  7. Click "6. Capture After Close" and take a final heap snapshot
  8. In DevTools, compare snapshots: Snapshot 3 - Snapshot 1 should show minimal retained memory
  9. Look for Monaco-related objects (search for "monaco", "editor", "model") - they should be minimal or zero
