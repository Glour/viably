---

**The Problem: The Automation Gap**

In 2024, businesses have two options for automation:

1. **No-code tools** like Zapier and Make—great for simple workflows, but limited when you need custom logic, complex integrations, or database operations.

2. **Hire developers**—expensive (€3,000-€5,000 per bot), slow (2-4 weeks), and requires ongoing maintenance.

Most small businesses and entrepreneurs fall into this gap: their needs are too complex for no-code tools but too small to justify hiring developers.

**The result?** Manual processes that should be automated, costing hours of productive time every week.

---

**The Solution: AI-Generated Production Code**

Viably bridges this gap by using AI (Claude Sonnet 4) to generate complete, production-ready Python code based on natural language descriptions.

Here's how it works:

1. **Choose a template** (Event Registration, Feedback Collection, Quiz Bot, Product Catalog, Survey Bot, or Notification Bot)

2. **Describe your requirements** in plain language—no technical jargon needed

3. **AI generates the code**—including error handling, database operations, validation, and tests

4. **Deploy in one click**—your bot goes live in 60 seconds on fully managed infrastructure

The key difference from traditional no-code tools: **you get real, extensible code that you own**. Download it, modify it, or let Viably manage it—your choice.

**Why Telegram Bots?**

Fair question—why not WhatsApp or standalone apps?

**Telegram offers unique advantages for business automation:**

1. **No approval process** - Deploy instantly without app store reviews
2. **Rich functionality** - Inline buttons, forms, payments, files
3. **Desktop + mobile** - Seamless cross-platform experience
4. **API-friendly** - Unlimited bot accounts, generous rate limits
5. **Privacy-focused** - End-to-end encryption available
6. **Growing adoption** - 900M+ monthly active users globally

For internal tools and community engagement, Telegram is often the fastest path to production.
