# Product Hunt Launch Content

## Pre-Launch Checklist

### 2 Weeks Before
- [ ] Reserve launch date in Product Hunt Ship
- [ ] Prepare all visual assets (screenshots, demo video)
- [ ] Set up PH account with complete profile
- [ ] Reach out to maker community for support
- [ ] Prepare hunter outreach list (if using a hunter)

### 1 Week Before
- [ ] Create teaser posts on Twitter/LinkedIn mentioning PH launch
- [ ] Email beta users asking for PH support
- [ ] Prepare FAQ document for comments
- [ ] Test demo environment thoroughly
- [ ] Write all launch content (below)

### Day Before
- [ ] Schedule launch for 12:01 AM PST
- [ ] Notify team/supporters of launch time
- [ ] Prepare response templates
- [ ] Clear schedule for launch day

### Launch Day
- [ ] Go live at 12:01 AM PST
- [ ] Post to Twitter/LinkedIn/Reddit immediately
- [ ] Respond to comments within 5 minutes
- [ ] Monitor rankings hourly
- [ ] Share milestones (50 upvotes, #1 in category, etc.)

## First Comment (Maker Introduction)

### Template

Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm [Your Name], and I'm excited to launch **Viably** today!

**TL;DR:** Viably uses AI to turn your ideas into production-ready Telegram bots—no coding required.

---

**🤔 Why I built this**

For the past 3 years, I've been freelancing as a developer building Telegram bots for small businesses. I kept seeing the same pattern:

- Entrepreneurs had great ideas for automation
- No-code tools couldn't handle their specific needs
- Hiring a developer cost $3,000-$5,000 and took weeks
- Most projects were 80% similar (registration, feedback, quizzes)

I thought: "What if I could automate... automating these bots?"

---

**⚡ How it works**

1. **Choose a template** - Event registration, quiz, survey, product catalog, feedback, or notifications
2. **Describe your needs** - "I want users to register for my webinar, get a confirmation email, and send a reminder 1 hour before"
3. **AI generates code** - Complete Python/aiogram code with database, error handling, and tests
4. **Deploy in one click** - Live in 60 seconds on fully managed infrastructure

---

**🎯 Who it's for**

- **Event organizers** - Automate registration, reminders, and feedback
- **Business owners** - Customer support, lead collection, notifications
- **Community managers** - Polls, quizzes, engagement tools
- **Educators** - Quizzes, assignments, student communication

Anyone who needs automation but doesn't want to learn to code or hire a developer.

---

**💡 What makes it different**

**vs. No-Code Tools (Zapier, Make):**
- Generates real code, not form builders
- No limits on complexity or customization
- You own the code and can download it

**vs. Hiring Developers:**
- 100x faster (minutes vs. weeks)
- 10x cheaper ($15/mo vs. $3,000+)
- No ongoing maintenance costs

**vs. Other AI Code Generators:**
- Specialized for Telegram bots (not generic)
- Includes deployment, not just code
- Production-ready quality, not prototypes

---

**🚀 Current features**

✅ 6 bot templates
✅ Natural language customization
✅ One-click deploy to Railway
✅ Download source code
✅ Real-time generation progress
✅ Credit-based pricing (no surprise bills)

**Coming soon (Q1-Q2 2026):**
- Payment processing bots
- WhatsApp & Slack support
- API service generation (not just bots)
- Team collaboration features

---

**💰 Pricing**

I deliberately avoided token-based pricing after being burned by surprise ChatGPT API bills.

Instead: **1 credit = 1 bot generation** (flat rate)

- **FREE:** 5 credits + 5 per referral
- **STARTER:** $15/mo for 100 credits
- **PRO:** $39/mo for 300 credits
- **BUSINESS:** $99/mo for 1,000 credits

---

**🎁 Product Hunt Special**

For PH community:
- **20 bonus credits** on signup (free)
- **Lifetime 20% off** any paid plan

Just mention "Product Hunt" when signing up.

I'll be here all day responding to comments and questions!

Thanks for checking out Viably. Your feedback and support mean everything. 🙏

**Try it:** https://viably.dev

## Comment Response Templates

### Positive Feedback
```
Thank you so much! 🙏

[Specific response to their comment]

If you try it out, I'd love to hear what you think. DM me and I'll send you extra credits to experiment with.
```

### Feature Request
```
Great idea! This is actually something [already on roadmap / we've been considering / haven't thought of yet].

[If on roadmap]: Coming in [timeframe]
[If considering]: What would your use case be for this?
[If new]: Can you tell me more about how you'd use this?

Adding this to our feature request tracker. Thanks for the suggestion!
```

### Pricing Question
```
Thanks for asking about pricing!

[Detailed breakdown of value]

The math: if a bot saves you even 1 hour/week at $50/hr, that's $2,600/year in value from a one-time $0.15 generation (STARTER plan).

Does that make sense for your use case?
```

### Comparison to Competitor
```
Great question about [Competitor]!

Main differences:
1. [Key difference 1]
2. [Key difference 2]
3. [Key difference 3]

But honestly, if [Competitor] works for you, use it! Different tools for different needs.

What's your specific use case? Might help me recommend which is better fit.
```

### Technical Question
```
Good technical question!

[Detailed answer with specifics]

The generated code uses [tech stack details]. You can inspect it before deploying.

Want to see an example? I can share a sample generated bot repository.
```

### Criticism/Concern
```
Appreciate the honest feedback!

[Acknowledge the concern]

You're right about [specific point]. Here's how I'm thinking about it: [explanation]

What would need to change for this to work for you?
```

```
❌ "Hey! Can you upvote Viably on Product Hunt?"

```

## Success Metrics

**Realistic goals:**
- 200+ upvotes
- Top 5 product of the day
- 50+ comments with engagement
- 100+ signups from PH traffic
- 5+ paying customers within 7 days

**Stretch goals:**
- 500+ upvotes
- #1 product of the day
- Featured in PH newsletter
- 500+ signups from PH traffic
- 20+ paying customers within 7 days

**What matters more than ranking:**
- Quality of conversations in comments
- Feature requests that validate product direction
- Connections with potential partners/advisors
- Press mentions from launch
- Long-term users (not just launch day spike)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Asking for upvotes directly
❌ Only responding to positive comments
❌ Being defensive about criticism
❌ Ignoring questions about pricing/tech
❌ Disappearing after first few hours
❌ Using fake accounts to upvote
❌ Launching without completing profile/gallery
❌ Posting "We're #1!" before it's confirmed
❌ Neglecting to thank supporters
❌ Forgetting to track UTM parameters

✅ Be present and engaged all day
✅ Respond thoughtfully to every comment
✅ Share authentic behind-the-scenes insights
✅ Thank supporters genuinely
✅ Take criticism as learning opportunity
✅ Focus on conversations over rankings
✅ Follow up with engaged users
✅ Track metrics properly

---

*Product Hunt Strategy: It's about community, not just rankings. Be authentic, engaged, and genuinely interested in feedback.*
